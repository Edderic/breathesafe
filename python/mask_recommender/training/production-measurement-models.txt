Production ARKit architecture vs direct traditional and PCA models

PYTHONPATH=python python -m mask_recommender.scripts.compare_production_measurements \
  --frame PRIVATE_TRAINING_FRAME --summary PRIVATE_MEASUREMENT_SUMMARY \
  --output PRIVATE_OUTPUT_FOLDER

This is the corrected three-way comparison requested by the user. It replaces the
matched five-ARKit-input logistic arm in compare_direct_measurements with the actual
production custom_lr + latent_nose_v1 code. It does not use already-deployed weights.
Each production model trains only on the other six paired people; using deployed
weights could leak evaluation participants' fit outcomes.

All three models train on the same six people and score identical test observations
for the seventh. Seven leave-person-out folds; 421 observations / 7 people in the
November 12 snapshot. Latest per-person paired measurements supply the face inputs.
The original observed mask perimeter is preserved and face-minus-mask total-perimeter
mismatch is recomputed when using the paired ARKit summary. The independent nose
path is compared with a latent preferred mask/style nose distance in centimeters.

Production ARKit calls train_custom_lr_with_split and _predict_custom_lr_probabilities
without modifying their optimization or inference math: Adam, 200 epochs, lr .01,
class_reweight=False, original row-weighted BCE and nose-offset regularization.
Both the perimeter quadratic and shared-strength squared nose mismatch are active.
Three initializations: seeds 42/43/44, with fold offset. No held-out threshold tuning.

Traditional and PCA fits are unchanged from the preceding direct-model experiment:
L2 logistic regression, C=1, deterministic lbfgs, original mask features/interactions,
equal-person training weights, six direct traditional predictors or PCA-3. Scaling
and PCA fit on unique training people only. Their private observation keys and
probabilities were verified identical to the earlier experiment in all three runs.

Different architectures and training-weight recipes are intentional here. Results
compare these pipelines; they do not isolate measurement modality as a causal factor.
No conversion into ARKit, no imputation, no outside training people, no uploads.

Equal-person-weighted metrics at fixed 50% cutoff, mean across initializations:

Top-3 and Top-5 are 6/6 for all three arms. Rates apply only to six people with a
positive fit label and rank existing tested observations, not the whole catalog.
Repeated user-mask tests may remain. This is a tiny conditional denominator.

PCA has slightly better equal-person threshold metrics; production ARKit has lower
log loss and ties PCA on Top-1. Seven independent people, dates of measurements vs
tests, mixed label sources and model choices limit interpretation. All results are
exploratory. Three initializations are not independent datasets or confidence
intervals. Neither anatomical measurement accuracy nor calibrated population fit
probabilities are established by this experiment.
