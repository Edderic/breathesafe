Three direct measurement models, restricted to complete paired participants

PYTHONPATH=python python -m mask_recommender.scripts.compare_direct_measurements \
  --frame PRIVATE_TRAINING_FRAME --summary PRIVATE_MEASUREMENT_SUMMARY \
  --output PRIVATE_OUTPUT_FOLDER

All three arms train only on the same six paired people and test the seventh,
rotating over all seven. There are no outside training people, imputed paths,
traditional-to-ARKit conversions, or tuning on held-out labels. The classifier is
a matched L2 logistic regression (C=1, deterministic lbfgs, max_iter=2000).
This differs from the production perimeter-plus-nose ARKit architecture.

Face inputs:
- Five measured ARKit paths: nose, strap, top cheek, mid cheek, chin.
- Six direct traditional dimensions: face width, face length,
  bitragion-subnasale arc, nose protrusion, nasal root breadth, nose bridge height.
- First three PCA components of standardized six traditional dimensions.
  Three components are fixed before outcome evaluation, not selected for best
  performance on the seven held-out people. No whitening.

Scalers and PCA fit once per unique training person; repeated test rows do not
change the PCA population. Categorical mask encoders and mask-perimeter scaler
also fit on training observations only. Same mask features across all arms:
fit family, style, strap type, observed perimeter and its square. Geometry terms
include linear and squared components, interactions with mask categories and
interactions with observed mask perimeter. These interactions allow different
rankings for different faces, rather than only shifting every mask score by the
same face-specific intercept. Feature counts differ across modalities despite
the matched recipe, so this is not proof that only measurement modality matters.

Training weights give each person equal total weight (average row weight one).
There is no additional class reweighting. Report test-weighted and equal-person
metrics at a fixed .5 cutoff. Top-k ranks existing test observations and is
conditional on at least one positive label. Deduplication uses source observation
IDs where present; repeated tests of the same user/mask can remain.

November 12 local snapshot: 7 people, 421 tests, 115 positive labels; 6 people
qualify for top-k. Latest paired measurements may differ in date from each other
and from fit tests. Two people contribute 296/421 observations. In each fold PCA
retains between 83.6% and 98.5% of training-person standardized variance.

PCA-3 has the best equal-person F1 and Top-1 in this particular baseline; raw
traditional has lower log loss. All log losses are poor and the independent
sample size is seven. Hyperparameters/representation could change the ordering.
Do not claim a generally superior measurement method or deploy from this run.
This experiment supersedes the conversion comparison for the user's intended
three-way question; keep the previous comparison clearly labeled as different.

Output HTML/JSON contains aggregate results and snapshot hashes. Participant IDs
and individual fit scores are in private_predictions.json only. Nothing is
uploaded, no production model is changed, and presentation slides are unchanged.
