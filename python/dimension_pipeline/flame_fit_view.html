Landmark placement guide

Tragion: notch at the upper attachment of the tragus near the ear canal. Subnasale: where the base of the nose joins the upper lip. Menton: lowest point of the chin. Inspect both profiles and the underside; use intermediate controls to trace the tape-contact route.
