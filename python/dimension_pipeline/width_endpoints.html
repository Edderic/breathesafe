Support and interpretation

Each candidate uses fixed dense-model identity, source-image/network-crop/skin-mask gates, bounded multi-view triangulation and at least three visible surface views. Raw surface support is required before attachment to the smoothed surface. Missing region candidates can mean that the maximum breadth is not observed. Local maxima can be biased by noise or region boundaries. A source-supported point is not automatically alare or zygion. These are soft-tissue contacts; underlying bone is not located from RGB images.
