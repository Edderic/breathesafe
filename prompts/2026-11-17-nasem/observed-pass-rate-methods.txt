Repeat-balanced descriptive pass rates

Shared implementation: app/services/dashboard_pass_rates_service.rb.
DashboardService.calculate_pass_rates delegates to this calculator. Original
per-test overall/by-mask/by-strap/by-style fields remain available. New fields:
- equal_person_mask: overall, by_mask, by_strap_type, by_style.
- equal_family_by_style: family-weighted style means, denominators and family rates.
- weighting: policy descriptions and excluded-record count.

Pair policy selected by the presenter:
For each canonical (user_id, mask_id), convert observed pass/fail to 1/0 and take
the mean. Average those pair rates, assigning every pair equal weight. One pass
plus one fail contributes 50%; we do not choose the latest or an any-pass result.
Within one mask this gives each person equal weight. Across all masks a person
can contribute multiple different pairs; it is not equal total weight per person.
Report pair/test/person counts and how many pairs contain mixed outcomes. Do not
invent an integer number of "passed pairs" when pair outcomes are fractional.

Family/style policy:
1. Compute pair rates as above.
2. Within a (person, fit family, style), average that person's pair rates across
   mask variants. Repeat tests of a variant cannot give that variant extra weight.
3. Average people within each family/style to get the family rate.
4. Average the observed family rates equally within each style.
These are means of unrounded rates, not means of formatted percentages. Counts
include families, person-family combinations, pairs, tests and unique people.
Untested families are omitted, not treated as failing. A missing family ID creates
a separate fallback family for each mask ID; missing families are not pooled.
A family spanning styles is evaluated separately within each style.

Input boundary:
Use the same canonical labeled records as the existing dashboard. Mask IDs and
metadata are resolved by FitTestsWithFacialMeasurementsService. Balanced views
exclude records with missing person/mask identity or unrecognized outcomes;
these exclusions are counted. Empty balanced denominators return NULL/N/A, not
zero. Existing mixed source labels and source-consolidation policy are retained.
Source-only tests, self-selection, modifications, donning differences and
nonindependence remain limitations. These are descriptive observed rates, not
population estimates, randomized comparisons, predictions or fit guarantees.

Appendix slide 28 data:
Nov 13 local development DB export, restricted to canonical labeled records.
1,142 tests, 293 positive labels (25.66% per test); 756 pairs across 54 people,
16.75% with equal pair weighting; 14 pairs have mixed outcomes. The slide shows
styles with at least 10 source tests; the evidence/dashboard retain smaller and
unknown groups. Other deck snapshots retain their separately dated public counts.
This cohort is not either recommender CV cohort. All columns here use the same
local records. No participant IDs are included in the committed aggregate file.

observed-pass-rate-evidence.json stores aggregates, input hash, calculator hash
and source. Participant-level rows stay in ignored tmp/dashboard-pass-rate-review.
The live production credentials were unavailable for a fresh full-record export;
this is explicitly a local snapshot. When deployed, the dashboard recalculates
from its own active dataset; these frozen numbers are not hard-coded into the app.
No deployment or model training is part of this change.
