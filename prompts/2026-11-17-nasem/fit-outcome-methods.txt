Fit-label definition and historical conversion check

This presentation describes project training labels, not a claim that all evidence
sources are equivalent completed formal fit tests. See the September companion:
prompts/2026-09-25-nasem/outcome-methods.md (not changed by this update).

Current implementation:
- Direct N95-mode exercise ratios: harmonic aggregation, positive at >=100;
  optional Normal breathing (SEALED) excluded. No factor-2 adjustment.
- N99-derived: estimate leakage using sealed-reference / filtration data;
  exclude Talking and sealed breathing; harmonic aggregation times 2; >=100
  positive. Imported Aaron Collins/OpenAeros data and contributed sealed
  observations supply references. The numerical reference adjustment and factor
  2 are project choices, not instrument-standard equivalence transformations.
- Qualitative: all recorded nonblank exercises pass, at least one explicit Pass.
  An all-Fail record without an explicit Pass currently becomes unknown in this
  pathway. This permissive completeness rule is not a full-protocol assertion.
- Failed user seal checks can supply proxy negatives. Passed checks supply no
  positive label. Negative overrides positive for conflicting sources on a test
  record; unknown records are excluded by default.
Incomplete exercises, zero factors and missing references need a label-quality
audit; these slides do not change services or retroactively recompute labels.

Historical scatter plot:
Source is the saved HTML aggregate table (cell 77) and scatter cell 80 in
python/mask_recommender/notebooks/inferring-n95-mode-fit-factors.ipynb.
The source uses an April 9, 2025 input snapshot and one author/wearer. It joins
repeated N99/N95 tests by mask and facial hair, excludes selected N95 tests by
author judgment, then harmonically aggregates exercises/test combinations by
mask. This is neither a paired simultaneous protocol nor held-out validation.

The original table has 36 mask aggregates. Exclude every aggregate containing
assigned score-1 observations from the notebook's not_good_fit_df output:
11 are pure assigned values, one mixes assigned and tested values. The remaining
24 aggregates have measured N95 and N99-based converted estimates. Do not reuse
the notebook's 0.83 sensitivity/0.89 specificity on this different subset.

We preserve the saved six-decimal scores, not newly computed raw-data results.
Exercise-level conversion estimates were capped at 200 before harmonic
aggregation. The notebook documents a factor-2 adjustment, but exact historical
runtime code cannot be recovered from outputs alone. Current production pools
filtration references differently. The figure does not validate today's exact
production converter and is unrelated to recommender fit-score predictions.

Bayesian association model:
log10(measured N95) ~ Normal(alpha + beta*log10(capped conversion estimate/100), sigma)
alpha ~ Normal(2,1), beta ~ Normal(0,1), sigma ~ HalfNormal(.75).
Eight measured aggregates at 200 are right-censored (likelihood P(score>=200)),
not treated as exact 200. Predictor clipping/measurement error is not modeled;
the capped conversion estimate is the defined predictor. Lognormal conditional
median trend is 10**mu, not the arithmetic mean score.

Four-chain random-walk Metropolis with MAP-Hessian proposal; 5,000 warmup plus
15,000 saved draws per chain. Adaptation stops after warmup. Seed 20261113.
Rebuild script requires split R-hat <1.01 and ESS >500 for all three parameters.
Evidence JSON records numerical posterior summaries, diagnostics, anonymous
pairs and SHA-256 hashes. No raw participant records are fetched.

Dark band: pointwise 95% credible interval for conditional median trend.
Light band: pointwise 95% posterior predictive interval for another mask
aggregate under this same model. Neither is a population validation interval;
filtration uncertainty, correlated tests, selection, predictor errors and
between-person generalization are not modeled. Positive association is not
proof of agreement, calibration, or accurate classification near 100.

Appendix slide 26 makes the implementation explicit:
S_j = 1/(1-E_j) for imported efficiency E_j; contributed sealed ratios enter
directly. S_bar is the arithmetic mean over pooled ratios (SQL UNION).
Let M be the largest retained N99 exercise factor.
S_star = S_bar when S_bar > M; otherwise S_star = M+1.
E = 1-1/S_star; L_i = 1-(1-1/F_i)/E; Fhat_i = 1/L_i.
Adjusted aggregate = 2*n / sum_i(1/Fhat_i); positive at >=100.
The explicit conditional is not equivalent to max(S_bar,M+1).

Appendix slide 27: exact seal-check evaluation order
1. Missing/empty data or invalid JSON -> incomplete.
2. Sizing Too big or Too small -> fail immediately, even with missing air answer.
3. Missing/blank sizing OR positive-pressure air answer -> incomplete.
4. Both present and air=A lot of air movement -> fail.
5. Sizing=Somewhere in-between too small and too big AND air=Some air movement
   or No air movement -> questionnaire pass.
6. Other combinations -> incomplete.
Training mapping: fail -> false; pass or incomplete -> NULL. No positive label
comes from this source. Negative-pressure responses are not read by the backend
service. An unrecognized nonblank sizing answer plus A lot of air movement still
fails at step 4; other unrecognized combinations reach step 6. This documents
current project classification, not a formal fit-test pass criterion.
