Paired person-cluster bootstrap with classifier refitting — November 15, 2026

Purpose
Slide 9 and appendix slide 32 now include variation in the people supplying
classifier training data. This replaces the earlier bootstrap that merely
resampled frozen held-out predictions. The earlier evidence and methods remain
archived as recommender-fixed-prediction-bootstrap-evidence.json and
recommender-fixed-prediction-bootstrap-methods.txt.

Data and paired resampling
Use the same private prepared training frame: 49 people, 1,086 observations.
Draw 49 original people with replacement 10,000 times, RNG seed 20261114.
A selected person's complete set of tests receives that person's multiplicity.
Apply the identical draw to perimeter-only and perimeter-plus-nose models and
all three initializations. All copies of an original person stay together in
that person's original held-out fold. No copy leaks from test into training.

Retain the original five GroupKFold assignments and category catalog. For each
draw and fold, independently initialize and train both classifiers with seeds
42/43/44 plus the one-based fold number. Use the production architecture,
200 full-batch Adam epochs, learning rate 0.01, no class reweighting, and the
original nose-offset regularization. Training binary cross entropy is weighted
by participant multiplicity and divided by the total resampled training-row
count. This equals explicitly duplicating each selected person's rows.

There are 10,000 x 5 x 2 x 3 = 300,000 independent bootstrap classifier fits,
plus 30 original-cohort reference fits. Computational batching gives each fit
its own parameters, optimizer moments and loss; it does not share learned
parameters or average gradients across fits. Seed banks and hyperparameters
remain fixed. Private batch caches make an interrupted run resumable; they do
not replace training with the original frozen out-of-fold predictions.

Evaluation
Pool weighted held-out observations from all five folds within each model and
initialization, compute each metric, then average the three metric values.
Do not average probabilities first or count seeds as independent datasets.
Classification threshold: probability >=0.5. Compute natural-log loss after
clipping the correct-class probability to [1e-15, 1-1e-15].

Report linear-interpolated 2.5th/97.5th percentiles as pointwise 95% bootstrap
intervals. Paired difference is nose minus perimeter: positive precision,
recall, NPV and F1 or negative log loss favors the nose model. Count strict
wins, losses and ties (absolute tolerance 1e-12, no relative tolerance).
Exclude undefined paired metrics and report their counts and denominators.
The completed aggregate JSON contains every metric's intervals and win counts.

Verification
Four focused pytest checks verify batched refitting against explicit production
training on repeated rows for both architectures, independence from other
models in a computational batch, weighted metrics, and superiority/tie rules.
Production comparisons use 200 epochs. The unresampled reference reproduces
the original classification metrics exactly to rounding; log-loss differences
are below 5e-8 from float32 operation ordering. Deck validation allows 1e-6.

Limitations
This is full CLASSIFIER retraining, not end-to-end imputation refitting.
The inherited global ARKit-imputation regression and prepared facial inputs
remain fixed. Its pre-CV fitting remains an important generalization limitation.
Original fold assignments and the known category catalog remain fixed too.
These intervals capture resampled training-participant multiplicities, not
new fold selection, independent imputation training, hyperparameter selection,
or new random initialization draws. Three prescribed seeds are not three
independent datasets.

CV training sets overlap. Bootstrap clusters do not remove that dependence.
Convenience sampling, mixed fit-label sources and repeated person-mask tests
remain limitations. Masks/families are held as observed, not independently
resampled. No simultaneous-coverage or multiplicity adjustment is applied.
Bootstrap win frequencies are not Bayesian posterior probabilities of model
superiority or calibrated hypothesis-test p-values. This remains exploratory
internal evidence, not a substitute for prospective participant validation.
