# Edderic Ugaddan

Edderic Ugaddan leads research and engineering at Breathesafe LLC. He develops MasqFit, an open-source iOS app that uses facial measurements and fit-related data to help people find respirators likely to fit, and Breathesafe, a web platform for respirator information and recommendations. With support from a 2024 Kanro grant, he has recruited participants, collected facial measurements and respirator fit data, developed predictive models, and brought MasqFit to the App Store. His current research explores how standard cameras, 3D reconstruction, and anatomical landmark detection could extend facial measurement to Android and the web, with independent validation of measurement accuracy and recommendation usefulness. He also leads engineering for an Inspired Air Fund dashboard that tracks classroom air quality and air cleaner data. Previously, he was a senior machine learning engineer at Dataplor and a data scientist at Panorama Education. He holds a BA in computer science from the University of Richmond.
